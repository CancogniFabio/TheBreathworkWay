The Breathwork Way Framework

A step-by-step process to regulate your system and reconnect with yourself.

layer by layer

The Breathwork Way is a breathwork-centred framework that brings together nervous system regulation and somatic depth, and I often describe it as an onion.
Over the years we all build up layers: habits, tension, protective patterns, emotions we never had time for, each one is there for a reason.

So we never go straight for the core but we start from the outside and move inward, one layer at a time listening to what your body shows us and what it is ready for.

I use this framework with the clients I work with in Lisbon and online.

Layer 01 · Stabilise

Regulate

Everything starts with knowing where you are.

We begin with an assessment of your nervous system: how you respond to stress, where you tend to get stuck and what your breath is already telling us.

If you've been running on high alert for a long time, this is where your system can finally start to settle. Together we find the practices and resources that work for you, so you always have a way back to calm and back to yourself when life gets stressful.

Reveal

This is often where we meet parts of you that have been carrying a story or an emotion for a long time. We approach them with curiosity and care, at your pace, building a real connection with your body and what it has been trying to tell you.

Different approaches to Breathwork

A structured breathwork approach bridging nervous system science, somatic psychology and leadership integration.

Activating Practices

Conscious connected breathing, Wim Hof breathwork and similar modalities use continuous rhythmic breathing to create non-ordinary states of consciousness.

Best for: Emotional release, deep processing, accessing subconscious material

Calming Practices

Pranayama, box breathing and vagal toning techniques use slower, more controlled breathing to down-regulate the nervous system.

Best for: Anxiety reduction, sleep improvement, daily regulation

Trauma-Informed Approach

Emphasises safety, choice and titration. Respects your system’s capacity and never forces experience. This is how I work.

Best for: Those with trauma history, high sensitivity or new to somatic work

Integrative Method

The Breathwork Way combines multiple modalities, adapting techniques to what your nervous system needs in each moment.

Best for: Comprehensive nervous system training and sustainable practice

Safety First.

Trauma-Informed Approach

→ You’re always in control. You can adjust, slow down, or stop at any time.

→ I explain what’s happening so you understand your experience.

→ We work within your window of tolerance, never forcing or pushing.

→ Your body’s wisdom guides the process, not my agenda.

→ Integration and pacing are prioritised over intensity.

Considerations

Breathwork is generally safe when practised with proper guidance. Some conditions need caution or medical clearance, and safety always comes first.

Talk to me before if you have:
  • Pregnancy
  • Cardiovascular conditions
  • Epilepsy or seizure history
  • Severe asthma
  • Recent surgery or injury
  • Psychosis or bipolar disorder
