Fix how I breathe

Breathing mechanics session

For you if

You breathe through your mouthYou often feel short of breathYou sigh a lotTight chest, jaw or shouldersYou want better breathing for sport

What happens

I test how you actually breathe: nose or mouth, rhythm, how your ribs and diaphragm move, how well you tolerate CO2. Then we retrain the pattern with simple drills. Very practical, a bit like physio for your breathing.

  • WhereLisbon or online
  • Length60 to 90 min
  • You leave withYour breathing drills

Go deeper

Conscious Connected Breathwork

For you if

Something feels stuckYou can't think your way out of itYou push emotions downYou've already done some inner work

What happens

You lie down and breathe in a continuous rhythm for about an hour, with music, while I guide you. Emotions, memories or strong body sensations can come up. We prepare together before, and take time after to make sense of what came up.

  • Length2 to 3 hours
  • WhereIn person, Lisbon

Make it last

6-week one-to-one program

For you if

You want change that staysYou've tried apps and they didn't stickYou want guidance week by week

What happens

Six private sessions over six weeks. The first is in person in Lisbon, the rest online. Between sessions you have a short daily practice, and we follow three steps in order: two weeks to regulate, two to reveal, two to release.

Had a single session first? Its cost comes off the program if you join within three months.

  • Length6 weeks
  • FormatA mix of online and in person

Three steps.
In this order.

The Breathwork Way is a structured approach that brings together nervous system science, breathing mechanics and somatic work.

01

Regulate

First, safety. We get your body out of survival mode with precise, gentle breathing and a daily practice you can keep.

02

Reveal

Then we look at your patterns: where the breath stops, where tension sits, how your system reacts under pressure.

03

Release

When your system is ready, deeper breathwork lets you move what thinking alone can't reach.

I never start with intense breathwork.
Regulation comes first.

who you will work with

Hey, I'm Fabio

For years I was a structural engineer and my job was to calculate loads, stress and how much a structure can carry before it cracks.

At some point I realised I was running the same calculation on myself, and the numbers didn’t look good. Breathwork brought me back into my body and now, five years later, it’s my work.

I still think like an engineer. I assess before I guide, I explain what’s happening in your nervous system, and I give you tools you can use on your own, long after our sessions end.
