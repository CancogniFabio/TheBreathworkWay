The Breathwork Way Framework

My approach is like peeling an onion: from the outside in, gently, listening to what your body is ready for.

01

Regulate

"Can I feel calm in my body again?"

First we bring your system out of alert, with gentle breathing and a daily practice that gives you a way back to calm.

02

Reveal

"Why does my body react like this?"

Then we listen to what the tension and the symptoms are saying, and understand the patterns behind them.

03

Release

"Can I finally let it go?"

When your body feels safe, deeper breathwork lets you release what thinking alone can't reach.
